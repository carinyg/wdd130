Excursion 1

Little River Valley

This delightful excursion takes you to the picturesque Little River Valley. Beginner level rapids make it ideal for large groups and beginners. Stop at a campground with rustic cabins and stay for the guided hiking trails to secluded waterfalls. Fun for everyone!

Excursion 2

Midvale Rapids

If you're group is ready for the next level, Midvale Rapids is for you. The desert campround has a rugged beauty you are sure to enjoy. The river provides burst of challenge balanced with tranquil opportunities to take in the sites.

Excursion 3

Raging River

For the experienced white water rafters we offer the Raging River Adventure. Certified participants will love the non-stop action. For those, seeking thrills, this excursion does not disappoint.
